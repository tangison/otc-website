# PRODUCT.md — Ongenga Technical College

## What this is
Production website for Ongenga Technical College (OTC), a private TVET institution in Omafo, Ohangwena Region, Namibia. Trading under Ethics Group Holdings (Pty) Ltd. Founded October 2019.

## Who it serves
- School leavers in Ohangwena and neighbouring regions deciding where to train.
- Parents and guardians comparing institutions.
- Working adults seeking short courses (ICDL, poultry farming, upholstery, millwright).
- Partners and sector bodies verifying OTC's standing.

## What the visitor must do
Enrol: pick a course, understand intakes and requirements, and contact the college by phone or email. There is no online payment or form submission yet (application form PDF not supplied).
