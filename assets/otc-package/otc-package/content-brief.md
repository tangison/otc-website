## 2. Vision, Mission, Values

**Vision:** To be Namibia's Best and preferred technical training institution.

**Mission:** To nurture the masters of value addition that will harness community development, enhance employability, entrepreneurship and innovation.

**Values:**
- Skills Development
- Entrepreneurship
- Community Development
- Inclusiveness

**About blurb (source: OTC profile PDF):**
Ongenga Technical College (OTC) is a privately conceived Technical Vocational Education and Training Institution, established by a young Namibian entrepreneur, trading under its mother company Ethics Group Holdings (Pty) Ltd. OTC aims to provide technical vocational education and training to every able-bodied Namibian from all walks of life, empowering them with the skills and knowledge that drive industrialization and socio-economic development. Its programs are aligned with Namibia's National Development Plans, the Harambee Prosperity Plan I & II, Vision 2030, and the UN Sustainable Development Goals.

## 4. Institutional Units

- **Entrepreneurial Development Centre (EDC):** Promotes innovation, creativity and entrepreneurship among trainees, students and the public. Helps trainees establish their own businesses with small start-up assistance.
- **OTC Boxing Club:** Promotes boxing as an adopted sport code for the institution; open to public participation/registration.
- **Auxiliary Centre:** Sells institutional corporate items and training accessories.
- **Sem Hasheela Agricultural Campus:** Agricultural incubation campus / production hub covering Apiculture (Bee Farming), Crop and Animal Husbandry, Food Processing, Mushroom Production.
